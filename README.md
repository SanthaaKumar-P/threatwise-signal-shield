ThreatZero Sentinel X is an AI Powered Autonomous Cyber Defence Platform that continuously protects enterprise networks through AI-powered threat detection, Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR), Extended Detection & Response (XDR), Explainable AI, MITRE ATT&CK Intelligence, NIST Cybersecurity Framework Mapping, AI SOC Copilot, Secure Digital Evidence Management, Executive Risk Analytics and Quantum-Ready Security.
